Undergraduate Scholarships in India

Compiled from recent publications (last 7 days as of 15 Nov 2026). Always verify on official portals.

1. ONGC Merit Scholarship 2026-27

Amount: ₹48,000 per year

Application Deadline: 30 November 2026

Eligibility: Indian nationals from SC, ST, OBC, or economically weaker General (EWS) categories. First year of full-time Engineering, MBBS, MBA, or Master’s in Geology/Geophysics. Minimum 60% marks or 6.0 CGPA in qualifying exam. Family income less than ₹4.5 lakh (SC/ST) or ₹2 lakh (OBC/General). Age not more than 30 years as on 1 August 2026. 50% seats reserved for girls. Apply via ongcscholar.org (NSP OTR required).

2. Vylor Agritech Scholarship FY 2026-27

Amount: ₹25,000 per year for UG

Application Deadline: 30 November 2026

Eligibility: Students in any year of UG (Agriculture and allied courses) at government institutions including IITs, NITs, IISc, ICAR-recognised colleges, and State Agricultural Universities. Annual family income not exceeding ₹6 lakh. Preference to female students. Apply on Buddy4Study.

3. HDFC Bank Parivartan ECSS Programme 2026-27

Amount: ₹30,000 (general UG) / ₹50,000 (professional UG)

Application Deadline: 15 December 2026

Eligibility: Indian nationals currently enrolled in recognised undergraduate courses. Minimum 55% in last exam. Family income up to ₹2.5 lakh. Preference for students facing recent family or personal crisis. Apply on Buddy4Study / official portal.

4. AICTE Pragati Scholarship Scheme 2026-27

Amount: ₹50,000 per year

Application Deadline: 30 November 2026 (as per recent NSP reports)

Eligibility: Girl students admitted to first year (or second year via lateral entry) of AICTE-approved technical degree/diploma courses. Maximum two girls per family. Family income up to ₹8 lakh. Apply on National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in).

5. AICTE Saksham Scholarship Scheme 2026-27

Amount: ₹50,000 per year

Application Deadline: 30 November 2026

Eligibility: Students with disability of at least 40% pursuing AICTE-approved technical courses. Family income up to ₹8 lakh. Apply on NSP.

6. AICTE Swanath Scholarship Scheme 2026-27

Amount: ₹50,000 per year

Application Deadline: 30 November 2026

Eligibility: Orphans, children of parents who died due to COVID-19, wards of armed forces/paramilitary personnel killed in action, or those with critically ill parents, in AICTE-approved courses. Family income up to ₹8 lakh. Apply on NSP.

7. Focus Area Science Technology Summer Fellowship

Amount: Fellowship opportunity

Application Deadline: 30 November 2026

Eligibility: Students pursuing B.Sc., B.Tech., BCA, MBBS, etc., with minimum average of 65% in core subjects from Class 10 onwards. Offered by IISc, INSA, and NASI.
